Revise the GOAT app design direction.

GOAT should not feel like a productivity app, data dashboard, or efficiency tool.
Travel is emotional, slow, visual, and mood-driven.

Design goal:
Make GOAT feel like a lightweight travel mood curation app.
The app should reduce decision fatigue, but it should not feel like it is forcing users to optimize their trip.

Core feeling:
- emotional
- visual
- calm
- travel-magazine-like
- postcard-like
- moodboard-like
- minimal but warm

Avoid:
- overly efficient/task-management feeling
- dashboard-like layout
- too many form-like steps
- too much data on the first view
- cold fintech-style UI
- generic AI app look

1. Landing screen
- Make the first impression emotional, not functional.
- Main message should feel like:
  "해외여행 같은 장면을 강원에서 찾아보세요"
  or
  "오늘 끌리는 감성으로 강원을 골라보세요"
- The primary CTA should be emotional:
  "오늘의 감성 찾기"
  or
  "지금 끌리는 장면 고르기"
- Keep explanations short and poetic.
- Do not make the app feel like a fast task completion tool.

2. Mood selection screen
- Mood cards should feel like postcards or travel magazine cards.
- Each mood should have:
  - mood title
  - short emotional scene phrase
  - 2~3 soft tags
- Example:
  "산토리니 감성"
  "흰 건물과 푸른 바다가 만나는 장면"
  "#오션뷰 #일몰 #이국적"
- Prioritize visual mood over dense information.

3. Travel preference screen
- Make preferences feel like light travel context, not a required form.
- Required fields should remain minimal.
- Use softer copy:
  "누구와 떠나나요?"
  "어떻게 이동하나요?"
  "오늘 여행의 목적은?"
- visitTime should be optional and feel like a gentle hint:
  "시간대도 정했다면 알려주세요"
- Avoid making the user feel tested or filtered.

4. Results screen
- Results should feel like curated travel cards, not ranked search results.
- Use card roles, but make them warm:
  - "가장 닮은 장면"
  - "비슷한 감성의 대안"
  - "오늘 가기 편한 선택"
- Each card should have one emotional reason first, then practical chips second.
- Practical info such as weather, access, safety should support the mood, not dominate it.

5. Place detail screen
- Detail should feel like opening a travel card.
- Top area must be visual.
- Order:
  1. hero visual
  2. emotional one-line description
  3. why this place matches the mood
  4. photo point
  5. gentle visit tips
  6. safety/access info
  7. KakaoMap CTA
- KakaoMap CTA should be clear but not aggressive.

6. Copywriting tone
Use warm Korean travel copy.
Avoid cold terms like:
- 최적화
- 효율
- 조건 기반
- 데이터 기반 추천
- 빠른 의사결정

Prefer:
- 감성
- 장면
- 오늘 끌리는
- 닮은 분위기
- 가볍게 떠나기
- 이국적인 순간
- 지금 가기 좋은

7. Overall visual style
- White or soft warm background
- Large mood cards
- More whitespace
- Fewer icons
- Softer buttons
- Travel-card visuals
- Avoid excessive gradients and startup-template UI
